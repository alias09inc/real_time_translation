Term: 'Arena Score' (asr_start=2408s)
  ASR:            Arena score.
  WITH dict:      Arenaスコアが
  WITHOUT dict:   アリーナスコア。そしてそれは (asr="arena score. And it's")

  "アリーナスコア" phonetically transliterates "arena" as the common noun
  (a stadium/arena), losing the fact that "Arena Score" is the proper name
  of the LMSYS Chatbot Arena leaderboard metric. "Arenaスコア" (with dict)
  preserves it as the named benchmark, matching how a Japanese ML audience
  would actually recognize the term.

  katakana transliteration ("token-per-second") that isn't standard
